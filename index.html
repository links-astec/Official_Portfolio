About

I'm an AI engineer from Ghana, now based in Tourcoing, France, where I'm doing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at JUNIA ISEN. Most of my work sits where models meet products: retrieval pipelines, LLM evaluation, and the APIs and interfaces around them.

This summer I was a research intern at Sheffield Hallam University, building NLP pipelines for LLM security research — measuring hallucinations, bias and guard-rail failures. Before that I was CTO of GLINAX in Ghana, and co-authored a paper on running a heart-disease screening model on an Arduino, with no internet connection.

I care about the unglamorous parts — chunking strategy, evaluation, deployment — because that's usually where a model succeeds or fails.

Research

Journal article · IJSET Vol. 11, Issue 6 · 2023

Design of a TinyML-Based Predictive Heart Disease Screening Device

Acquah Andrews, Quaye Nii Attoh Gabriel — Computer Engineering, KNUST

Cardiovascular disease is a leading global health concern. This study deploys a shallow neural network on an Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense microcontroller to predict heart failure from eight clinical features. It runs entirely on the device — no internet connection required — which makes it viable in clinics with limited infrastructure.

82.61%accuracy
