Research

Design of a TinyML-Based Predictive Heart Disease Screening Device

A small neural network on an Arduino that screens for heart failure from eight clinical measurements, with no internet. It reached 82.61% accuracy and a ROC-AUC of 92.15%. Published in 2023 with Acquah Andrews at KNUST, in the International Journal of Science, Engineering and Technology (vol. 11, no. 6).
